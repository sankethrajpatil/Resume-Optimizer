Now Brewing – product manager, Inventory Excellence! #tobeapartner

From the beginning, Starbucks set out to be a different kind of company. One that not only celebrated coffee and the rich tradition, but that also brought a feeling of connection. We are known for developing extraordinary leaders who share this passion and are guided by their service to others.

We are hiring Product Managers to lead the development and implementation of Starbucks' new Coffeehouse Inventory Playbook, a core initiative that connects Inventory Operational Excellence with our Back to Starbucks Availability strategy. In these roles, you will drive the innovation, strategy, and roadmap for solutions that strengthen inventory practices across our coffeehouses. You will co-create and integrate new technologies into our operational ecosystem to simplify in-store execution, enhance partner workflows, and improve customer experiences. Additionally, you will help standardize and elevate best practices across stores to ensure consistent execution and reliable product availability, ultimately supporting a more seamless and efficient coffeehouse operation.

A product manager contributes to Starbucks' success by managing the development of new products and strategic opportunities against operating plans and budgets to achieve customer and business objectives.

As a product manager in Coffeehouse Excellence, you will…

Serve as the single-threaded owner for product outcomes, driving coordination and ensuring product work is owned and supported by the right teams.

Own the product strategy, roadmap, and vision, aligning with business goals and partner needs.

Gather and prioritize customer and partner needs, use cases and feedback into actionable requirements and success metrics.

Define required features and outcomes, using data and insights to guide product decisions.

Collaborate closely with the program manager to align on timelines, resource allocation and strategic priorities across initiatives.

Partner with operations consultation managers on implementation while maintaining accountability for product outcomes.

Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to ensure solutions deliver on intended business outcomes.

Facilitate decisions by balancing risk, available information and shifting priorities.

We'd love to hear from people with:

Bachelor's or equivalent related work experience or four years US military experience in a related MOS/C

Strong interpersonal skills

Ability to communicate clearly and concisely, both orally and in writing

Ability to work both independently and as part of a team

Ability to confidently present objectives and desired outcomes to leadership and cross functional teams

Ability to resolve issues or debates independently in most situations

Ability to balance multiple priorities and meet deadlines

2-3 years of progressive experience in product or brand management preferably with a focus on operations, process improvement, or customer/partner experience

Ability to influence others without authority to complete things in a timely manner
